Delete your GST Billing account

You can permanently delete your GST Billing account and all data linked to it at any time.

Option 1 — from the app (instant)

  1. Open GST Billing and go to More.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Option 2 — by email (if you no longer have the app)

Send an email to support@selectionlab.in from the same Google email address you used to log in, with the subject "Delete my GST Billing account". We will delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your login account
  • Business profile, logo and signature
  • Bills, quotations, challans, purchases, returns, payments, expenses, parties and items
  • Cloud sync data and daily backups
This cannot be undone. Download, share or print any bills you need before deleting. Records of payments made to us, if any, may be kept only for the period required by Indian tax laws.
