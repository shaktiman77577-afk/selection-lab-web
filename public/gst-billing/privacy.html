GST Billing ("the app") is made by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address from Google Sign-In, used to create and identify your account.
  • Business information you enter: business name, address, state, GSTIN, PAN, phone, bank and UPI details, invoice settings, and your business logo and signature images (only if you add them).
  • Records you create: bills, quotations, challans, purchases, returns, payments, expenses, parties (your customers and suppliers, including their names, phone numbers, addresses and GSTIN) and items.
  • Device information: device model, app version and a random ID created by the app, used to keep your account active on one phone at a time and to show backup status.
  • Photos: the app accesses your photos only when you choose a logo or signature image.

We do not collect your contacts, location, call logs, SMS or any data unrelated to your billing.

2. How we use it

  • To create invoices and other documents, calculate GST and keep your accounts.
  • To save your data securely in the cloud and bring it back when you log in on a new phone.
  • To keep your account signed in on only one phone at a time.
  • To provide support when you contact us.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with advertisers.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on your phone and in our cloud database and storage provided by Supabase. Changes are synced to the cloud a few seconds after you save them, and one full daily backup is kept (older backups are deleted automatically). Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each account can access only its own data.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In / Firebase — to log you in.
  • Supabase — cloud database, file storage and server functions.
  • WhatsApp and other apps you choose — only when you share a bill or statement yourself.

These services handle data under their own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your account, business data, synced records and backups are deleted. Records of payments made to us, if any, may be kept for the period required by Indian tax laws.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit or delete your bills, parties and items in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account and all your data from the app: More → Account → Delete account, or by following the steps on this page.
  • You can contact us to access or correct your data.

7. Children

The app is meant for businesses and is not directed at children under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Important changes will be shown in the app.
